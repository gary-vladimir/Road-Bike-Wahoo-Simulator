## Physical versus virtual descent

KICKR CORE 2 uses electromagnetic resistance ([Wahoo specifications](https://support.wahoofitness.com/hc/en-us/articles/28096790110354-KICKR-CORE-2-Smart-Trainer-Information-FAQ)). Descending SIM requests reduce braking; BikeSIM does not request negative power or rely on a powered flywheel descent. Virtual gravity can keep the bike moving while the physical trainer spins down. Trainer minimum load limits physical downhill feel; ROUVY also describes this distinction in its [physics and trainer limitations](https://support.rouvy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018681117-How-does-ROUVY-control-resistance-and-calculate-virtual-power).
